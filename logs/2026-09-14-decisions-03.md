### When changes take effect

| Admin changes… | Takes effect |
|---|---|
| Monthly price | **At each existing member's next renewal.** The current paid month is unchanged. New sign-ups pay the new price immediately. |
| Discount % | Immediately, for all future pricing |
| Free-play allowance | At each member's next monthly grant |

**Implementation of price changes.** Stripe Prices can't be edited. The back office creates a new Stripe Price, then updates every active subscription on that tier with `proration_behavior: none`. Stripe then charges the new price from the next invoice. The back office also sends members an automatic "your price changes from {date}" email. ⚠️ This is good practice under Australian Consumer Law; confirm you want it.

## D22 — Free-play balance (supersedes D15 assumptions)

**Decisions:**
1. **Allowance per tier**, editable (D21).
2. **Unused minutes roll over and build up.** The balance never resets.
3. **A grant happens on each successful monthly payment.** On Stripe `invoice.paid`, including the first payment, the tier's allowance is added to the ledger.
4. **Free minutes cover the first minutes of a session.**
5. **Usable on any resource type**, both **online and at the POS**.
6. The member chooses how many minutes to use (up to the whole balance). The website and the POS both ask before using any.

The model stays as a ledger (`member_balance_ledger`, balance = sum of entries). Rollover means nothing needs to change, because there is no reset job.

**POS safeguard.** When a QR is scanned, the POS shows the **member's name and tier**. Before using any balance, the cashier confirms the name with the customer. This stops someone with a screenshot of another member's QR from spending that member's minutes.

⚠️ **Still to decide:**
- **Balance when payment fails or membership ends:** keep it (frozen, and usable again if they pay or re-join) or forfeit it? **Recommend: frozen while payment is failing, forfeited 30 days after the membership fully ends.** With rollover, members can build up a lot of minutes, so losing them instantly would feel harsh.
- **Upper limit on the balance:** with no cap, a Diamond member who doesn't visit for a year could hold 12× the allowance. This is effectively unpaid play the venue owes. **Recommend:** an optional `max_balance_minutes` per tier, left blank (no cap) at launch, so you can switch it on later without a code change.

## D24 — Launch happy hour

**Monday–Friday, 10:00am–3:00pm, 10% off.** Days, times and % are editable, and happy hour can be switched off.

⚠️ **Assumed:** applies to **all three resource types**. Separate happy hours per type can be set later in the back office.

**Worked example.** Gold member, driving sim, Wednesday 2:30–3:30pm:

| Segment | Minutes | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2:30–3:00pm (happy hour) | 30 | $54/hr | $27.00 |
| 3:00–3:30pm | 30 | $60/hr | $30.00 |
| **Subtotal** | | | $57.00 |
| Gold 10% | | | −$5.70 |
| **Total** | | | **$51.30** (GST $4.66) |

**Rounding rule (new):** the engine calculates in exact decimals and rounds **once**, on the final total, to the nearest cent (half-up). GST = total ÷ 11, rounded to the cent.

## D25 — Referral codes (updates D12)

- **Discount type:** percentage **or** fixed dollar amount, chosen per code.
- **Expiry date:** optional `valid_until`, set per code.
- **Max uses:** set per code. A code is rejected once it is expired, used up or deactivated, whichever comes first.
- **Usable online and at the POS.**
- **A use is NOT restored if a booking is refunded.** This will be revisited later.
- **Members can't use referral codes.** A logged-in or scanned member never sees the referral field (D9 confirmed).

**Fixed-amount math.** Happy hour applies to the rate first, then the fixed amount comes off the subtotal, and the total can't go below $0:
```
total = max(0, subtotal − fixed_amount)
```
Example: billiard table, 1 hour during happy hour = $27.00, with a $5 code = **$22.00**.

**Validation:** percentage must be > 0 and < 100. Fixed amount must be > 0.

## D26 — Selling memberships: online and at the POS

A monthly subscription needs a card saved **in Stripe**. The CommBank terminal can't do that, so both channels finish in Stripe Checkout:

- **Online:** Sign up → create account (email + password) → pick tier → Stripe Checkout (subscription) → active, QR issued.
- **At the POS:** cashier enters the customer's name + email and picks a tier → **POS shows a QR code on screen** → customer scans it with their phone → pays in Stripe Checkout on their own phone → membership activates, and the customer gets a "set your password" email plus their member QR.

⚠️ Confirm this POS flow. The alternative is emailing the customer a payment link, which is slower and easy to abandon at the counter.

## D27 — Member identification: QR in the venue, login online

The two methods answer the same question ("is this person a member?") in two different places:

| | QR code | Email + password |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | At the counter (POS) | On the booking website |
| **How** | Cashier scans the QR on the member's phone | Member types their login |
| **Why it works there** | Fast, no typing, and a human sees the customer | No human involved, so identity must be proven |
| **Why it fails in the other place** | Online, the member is booking on the same phone that shows the QR, so there's nothing to scan it with. A QR screenshot can also be shared, and with no cashier checking, anyone could book as that member and spend their balance. | At the counter, typing a password on the POS is slow and exposes the password to staff |

**Decision:** use **both**.
- The member QR is **digital**: shown in the member's account page and in the welcome email. No plastic cards at launch (Apple/Google Wallet pass stays in the Phase 8 backlog).
- Fallback at the POS: the cashier searches by phone or email if the member doesn't have their QR.
- The QR contains only a random token (D8), so it can be reissued if leaked.

## D28 — Membership payment failure, cancellation and tier changes (updates D17)

- **Payment fails → cut off immediately.** Status becomes `past_due`: no discount, no balance use, and the POS shows "Inactive — payment failed" when scanned. Stripe keeps retrying the card automatically. If a retry or a card update succeeds, the member is **reinstated automatically**.
- If all retries fail, Stripe cancels the subscription and the membership ends.
- **Member cancels:** ⚠️ assumed benefits continue until the end of the month already paid for (`cancel_at_period_end`), then end.
- **Upgrade/downgrade mid-month:** ⚠️ **recommend it takes effect at the next renewal**, the same rule as price changes. There's no proration and no part-month charge. Balance already earned is kept.

## D31 — Cancellation, refund, no-show and overstay policy ⚠️ PROPOSED

Drafted by the developer, based on: full payment upfront, short sessions, a 7-day booking window and a walk-in venue.

### Cancellation by the customer
Customers cancel through a link in the confirmation email. Refunds go back to the original card automatically through Stripe.

| Time before booking start | Money refunded | Free-play minutes | Referral use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **24 hours or more** | 100% | Returned | Not restored (D25) |
| **2–24 hours** | 50% | Not returned | Not restored |
| **Under 2 hours / no-show** | None | Not returned | Not restored |

### Cancellation by the venue
Equipment fault, closure, double-booking, etc.: **always 100% refund and all minutes returned**, whatever the timing. Australian Consumer Law requires a remedy when the venue fails to provide the service.

### No-shows and late arrivals
- A booked slot is **held for 15 minutes** after its start time. After that the cashier can mark it **no-show**, which frees the resource for walk-ins. No refund.
- **Late arrival:** the session still **ends at the booked end time**. Late customers lose the minutes they missed.

### Walk-ins vs upcoming bookings
- Online bookings can't start **less than 30 minutes from now** ⚠️. This gives staff time to wrap up a walk-in on that resource.
- A POS resource tile shows **"Next booking 4:00pm"**. When opening a walk-in, the cashier sees how long the resource is free.
- The POS alerts staff **10 minutes before** a booking starts on a resource that has a walk-in running.

### Overstay (booked customer plays past their end time)
- **If the resource is free afterwards:** the cashier can extend. Extra minutes are billed **at the POS** at the normal per-minute rate, with happy hour, membership and balance all applying as usual.
- **If the next slot is booked:** the session must end at the booked end time.

### Admin override
The superadmin can issue any refund, full or partial, from the back office at any time. Every override requires a reason and is written to the audit log.

**Note on Stripe fees:** Stripe does not return its processing fee when a payment is refunded. The venue absorbs about 1.7% + $0.30 on each refunded booking.

## D32 — Payments hardware, scanner, offline

- **Card terminal: CommBank.** For the launch build it **doesn't matter**. The POS doesn't talk to the terminal: the cashier types the amount into the CommBank terminal and records "card" in the POS.
  - It would only matter for a Phase 8 integration, where the POS sends the amount to the terminal so it can't be mistyped. CommBank terminals are generally less open to custom POS integrations than Tyro, Square or Zeller.
  - **To catch mistyped amounts:** shift close shows the POS **card total**, which staff compare against the CommBank terminal's end-of-day settlement total. Any difference is recorded as a variance, the same as cash (D7).
- **QR scanner** ⚠️ depends on what device the POS runs on:

  | | USB/Bluetooth scanner | Device camera |
  |---|---|---|
  | Hardware | Handheld 2D scanner, ~A$50–150. Must be **2D** and able to read phone screens. | None, uses the built-in camera |
  | Speed | Instant, point and beep | Slower; customer holds their phone up to the camera |
  | Reliability | Very high, handles screen glare well | Struggles with glare, low light and cracked screens |
  | Setup | Plug in. It types like a keyboard, no drivers | Browser camera permission |
  | Best for | Laptop/desktop POS | Tablet POS (iPad) |

  **Recommendation:** buy a 2D USB scanner if the POS is a laptop or desktop. The camera is also built in as a fallback, so both work either way.
- **Offline:** a **4G failover router** is accepted (Q12). The POS is online-only, with no offline mode.

## Updated pricing algorithm (replaces the version in Decisions Log #2)

```
INPUT: opened_at, closed_at, resource_type,
       member? | referral?              (never both — D9, D25),
       use_balance_minutes?             (member only, ≤ available balance — D22),
       rate_bands[], happy_hours[]

1. billable_period = [opened_at, max(closed_at, opened_at + min_minutes)]   # D16
   duration rounded up to whole minutes

2. free_period = first use_balance_minutes of billable_period               # D22
   paid_period = billable_period − free_period

3. segments = split(paid_period, rate-band + happy-hour edges,
                    evaluated in Australia/Sydney)                          # D5

4. subtotal = Σ segment_minutes × (base_rate × (1 − hh_pct if in HH)) / 60  # D2, D24

5. if member:                  total = subtotal × (1 − tier.discount_pct)
   elif referral is pct:       total = subtotal × (1 − referral.pct)
   elif referral is fixed:     total = max(0, subtotal − referral.amount)    # D25
   else:                       total = subtotal
   (no cap — D10)

6. total = round_half_up(total, cents);  gst = round_half_up(total / 11, cents)   # D24

7. emit explanation[]  — per segment, free minutes used, balance remaining, discount
```
